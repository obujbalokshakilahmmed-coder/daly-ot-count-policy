Privacy Policy for Daly Ot Count

Last Updated: October 23, 2026

MS Tech System ("we," "our," or "us") operates the Daly Ot Count mobile application (the "App"). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our App and the choices you have associated with that data.

By downloading and using Daly Ot Count, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. Since our App is built primarily as a secure, offline utility for workers, we respect your data sovereignty by keeping your records strictly on your device.

1. Information Collection and Use

For a better experience, while using our App, we may require you to provide us with certain personally identifiable information. The information that we request will be retained by us and used as described in this privacy policy.

A. Personal Data & Profile Identity

  • No Login or Account Requirement: Daly Ot Count DOES NOT require or collect Mobile Phone Numbers, Email Addresses, or Passwords to function. The App runs entirely without requiring account registration.
  • Custom Profile Customization: The App allows you to manually input profile fields such as Factory/Company Name, Worker Name, Card Number, Basic Gross Salary, and Attendance Bonus Rate. This text data is stored 100% locally on your device (SharedPreferences).
  • Profile Photo Storage: When you customize your profile image from the device gallery, the photo is copied into the App's protected Internal Storage directory. We do not transmit or upload your photos to external cloud servers.

B. Financial, Overtime & Duty Records

  • Local Database Encryption: All daily duty trackers, overtime (OT) hours, night bills, attendance records, and advance loan adjustments are securely stored inside an offline SQLite database local to your device.
  • No External Synchronization: Financial calculations, income projections, and salary log histories are processed directly on your device CPU. No operational data is sent to central servers.

2. Device Information & Technical Logs

We may process basic non-identifiable technical data sent whenever you utilize our services, including operating system versions, device models, app performance metrics, and layout scaling adjustments to verify smooth UI operations.

3. Advertising & Google AdMob Integration

This App incorporates third-party advertising services provided by Google AdMob (Google LLC) to serve advertisements. AdMob may collect and use pseudonymous device identifiers, IP addresses, and cookie data to deliver personalized or non-personalized banner and interstitial advertisements within the App.

AdMob Data Usage & Opt-Out:

  • Advertising ID: AdMob utilizes your Android Advertising ID to display relevant ads and limit ad repetition (Frequency Capping).
  • Personalized Ads: Depending on your device settings and user region, Google AdMob may customize ads based on your general preferences. You can opt out of personalized interest-based advertising through your Android OS settings via Settings > Google > Ads > Opt out of Ads Personalization.
  • Third-Party Vendor Policy: To learn more about how Google handles user privacy and advertising data, please review the official policy link: Google Advertising Privacy & Terms.

5. Data Security & Storage Isolation

The security of your overtime and financial ledger is highly important to us. Local preference sheets and SQLite database structures are restricted using MODE_PRIVATE isolation to guarantee neighboring phone applications cannot access or modify your personal wage logs.

6. User Rights & Data Wipe

Because all data is stored directly inside your device memory, you maintain full control over your information. You can permanently wipe or delete all overtime records, profile names, and cached photos at any moment by navigating to your device OS Settings > App Info for Daly Ot Count, and executing "Clear Data / Wipe Storage".

7. Children's Privacy

These Services do not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13 years of age.
