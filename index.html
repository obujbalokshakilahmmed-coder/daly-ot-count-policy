Privacy Policy for Daly Ot Count

Last Updated: September 14, 2026

MS Tech System ("we," "our," or "us") operates the Daly Ot Count mobile application (the "App"). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our App and the choices you have associated with that data.

By downloading and using Daly Ot Count, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. Since our App is built primarily as a secure, offline utility for workers, we respect your data sovereignty by keeping your records strictly on your device.

1. Information Collection and Use

For a better experience, while using our App, we may require you to provide us with certain personally identifiable information. The information that we request will be retained by us and used as described in this privacy policy.

A. Personal Data & Profile Identity

  • No Login or Account Requirement: Unlike other platforms, Daly Ot Count DO NOT require or collect Mobile Phone Numbers, Email Addresses, or Online Passwords to function. The App runs entirely without requiring account registration.
  • Custom Profile Customization: The App allows you to manually input profile custom fields such as Factory/Company Name, Worker Name, Card Number, Basic Gross Salary, and Attendance Bonus Rate. This text data is stored 100% locally on your device within secure private storage (SharedPreferences).
  • Permanent Profile Photo: When you customize your profile image from the device gallery, the App utilizes a permanent local file saver engine that copies the photo into the App's protected Internal Storage directory. We do not transmit or upload your photos to external cloud servers.

B. Financial, Overtime & Duty Records

  • Local Database Encryption: All manually input daily duty trackers, overtime (OT) hours, night bills, attendance records, and advance loan adjustments are securely stored inside an offline SQLite database local to your device.
  • No External Synchronization: Financial calculations, income projections, and salary log histories are processed directly on your device CPU. No operational data is sent to external or central servers.

C. Device Information

We may also capture standard hardware data sent whenever you utilize our services, including operating system versions, device names, app configurations during background state, runtime error/crash stack traces via offline logs, and performance statistics to verify layout scaling (such as bottom bar constraint adjustments).

3. Data Security & LifeCycle Execution

The security of your overtime and financial ledger is highly important to us. The App architecture incorporates native Android lifecycle state-locks (`onResume` UI sync hooks) that dynamically reload dashboard structures in memory safely. Local preference sheets are restricted using `MODE_PRIVATE` isolation to guarantee neighboring phone applications cannot spy on or modify your personal wage logs.

4. User Rights and Data Autonomy

We believe in full transparency and total user autonomy. Because all data is stored directly inside your device memory, you maintain full possession of your information. You can permanently wipe, clear, or delete all overtime records, profile names, and cached photos at any moment by navigating to your device OS Settings, selecting App Info for Daly Ot Count, and executing "Clear Data / Wipe Storage".

5. Children's Privacy

These Services do not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13 years of age. In the case we discover that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we immediately isolate and delete this from our local internal registers.
